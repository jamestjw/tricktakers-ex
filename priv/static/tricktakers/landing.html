Anatomy of a round
01 / DEAL
Five cards, four colors

Black is trump. Same-color tricks resolve high-to-low; off-color cards can’t win unless Black.

02 / CONTRACT
Pick a character

Each player chooses a different role with its own scoring rule and ability. Your character is your strategy.

03 / CROWN
Take tricks — or none

Most tricks earns a Crown. Zero tricks earns a Black Crown. First to two crowns wins the game outright.
