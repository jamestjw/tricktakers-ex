The roster

Eight characters, eight games.

Each character contracts a unique style of trick-taking — its own scoring, its own components, its own way to win the game outright. Use the priority order (1A → 5B) when characters tie or compete on the same condition.

Ability
After everyone sets up, hand one Task card to each opponent. They score the task's points; Ruler scores the same. Cooperative.
Difficulty
Each task has a Normal side (+10 / −10) and a Hard side (+20 / −10). Choose when assigning.
Tasks
All tasks completed · Take a Black card · Win only 1 trick · Win 2+ tricks with no Red/Blue/Green → win the game.
Win
All players (Ruler included) complete their tasks in the same round.
Distribute tasks based on opponents' characters: "take" tasks to King & Berserker, "do not take" to Hermit & Resistance.
