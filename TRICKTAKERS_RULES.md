# Tricktakers Rules

## Goal
Win by one of these paths:
- Meet a character win condition during a round.
- After crowns are awarded, have 2 gold crowns or 3 black crowns.
- If no immediate or crown victory occurs by the final round, use the final point total as the fallback winner.

## Trick Basics
- A trick is one card from each player, played clockwise.
- The lead player may play any card.
- Other players must follow the lead suit if they can.
- If you cannot follow suit, you may play any card.
- Highest strength card wins the trick.
- If tied, the first card played wins.
- The trick winner takes all played cards, faces them down, and leads the next trick.

## Card Strength
- Number cards: higher number is stronger within the same suit.
- Red, Blue, Green: the lead suit is stronger than the other two suits.
- Black: trump suit, stronger than Red/Blue/Green.
- Rare cards: stronger than all number cards, colorless, and can be played anytime.
- White Flag cards: weaker than all number cards, colorless, and can be played anytime.
- If a Rare or White Flag is led, the next non-Rare/non-White Flag card determines the lead suit.
- Black does not need to be "broken" before it can be led.

## Revolt Trick
- The Resistance can trigger a Revolt once per round by placing the Revolt token on the card they just played.
- During a Revolt Trick, the lowest strength card wins instead of the highest.
- Red, Blue, and Green lose their lead-suit advantage during a Revolt Trick.
- Black loses to Red/Blue/Green during a Revolt Trick.
- Hermit's White Flag bonus and Berserker's 1s bonus are also nullified during a Revolt Trick.
- If multiple cards tie for lowest, the first played wins.

## 3-5 Player Setup
1. Give each player 30 points.
2. Shuffle the 40 playing cards and deal 5 to each player.
3. Set the remaining points as the common supply.
4. Choose a lead player.
5. Place the 5 basic character cards face up.
6. Each player selects a character in character selection order.
7. Set up characters in character priority order.
8. Play 5 tricks.
9. Check character win conditions. If any player fulfilled their character win condition, that player wins immediately.
10. If no character wins, award crowns, points, and the lead player token.
11. Check for crown victory.

## 3-5 Player Round Flow
- Round 1: character selection starts clockwise from the lead player.
- Round 2 and Round 3: selection order depends on crowns and points.
- After each round, the player with the most tricks gets a gold crown. If two or more players tie for the most tricks, no one gets a gold crown.
- Players with 0 tricks are eligible for black crowns. Up to 2 players can receive black crowns.
- If 3 or more players are eligible for black crowns, the 2 eligible players with the highest character priority receive them.
- Players gain or lose points based on their characters.
- The player with the fewest points and no gold crown gets the lead player token.
- After crowns are awarded, any player with 2 gold crowns or 3 black crowns wins immediately.
- Otherwise, after Round 3, the player with the most points wins.

## Basic Characters

### King
- Setup: add the King Rare card to your hand, then discard any other card.
- Win condition: win all 5 tricks.
- Points: 0 tricks = +0, 1 trick = +20, 2 tricks = +50, 3 tricks = +80, 4 tricks = +120.
- Round 3: score double trick-based points only.
- Next round, you choose a character first, but you may not choose the King again.

### Gambler
- Setup: immediately gain 20 points.
- Setup: before bidding, you may perform up to 2 redraw actions.
- Redraw action: choose any number of cards from your hand, discard those cards, then draw the same number of cards so your hand returns to 5 cards.
- Setup: after you finish any redraw actions, use the Bidding Card to declare how many tricks you will win and choose your wager.
- Win condition: bid 4 tricks and win exactly 4 tricks, or win all 5 tricks.
- Points: 0 bid = +30, 1 bid = +60, 2 bid = +90, 3 bid = +150.
- If the bid is made, add the wager to the bid payout. If the bid is missed, subtract the wager from the bid payout.
- Round 3: you may bet up to 100 points instead of 50.

### Resistance
- Setup: place the Revolt token next to your character card.
- Once per round, before/while playing a card, you may trigger a Revolt Trick by placing the token on that card.
- Win condition: win the Revolt Trick with a Black card.
- If your only trick win is the Revolt Trick, you can still gain a black crown.
- Points: gain 30 points per trick you win, including the Revolt Trick.
- Revolt bonus: if you win the Revolt Trick with 7/8/9, gain +100; with 4/5/6, gain +80; with 1/2/3, gain +50; with White Flag, gain +30.

### Hermit
- Setup: discard all 5 cards, then add the Berserker card and 4 random cards from the Berserker deck.
- Before playing a card, you may draw 1 card and then discard 1 card from your hand.
- Your White Flag is stronger than Rare cards, except during a Revolt Trick.
- Win condition: win all 5 tricks.
- Points: 0 tricks = +50, 1 trick = -10, 2 tricks = -30, 3 tricks = +70, 4 tricks = +100.
- Round 3: score double points.

### Berserker
- Setup: discard all 5 cards, then add the Berserker card and 4 random cards from the Berserker deck.
- The Berserker card is stronger than Rare cards.
- Red 1, Blue 1, Green 1, and Black 1 are stronger than the Berserker card and stronger than the 10 of the same suit.
- Win condition: in round 3 only, win 0 tricks.
- Points: 0 tricks = -30, 1 trick = -10, 2 tricks = +30, 3 tricks = +50, 4 tricks = +80, 5 tricks = -50.
- Round 3: if you spend a black crown, you may discard 1 or 2 cards and draw only from the 2 unselected Berserker cards.

## Advanced Characters

### Adventurer
- Setup: choose 1 blue and 1 red initial item.
- You may use 1 available item before playing a card.
- When you win a trick, the won cards become a new equipment card and immediately get a new item.
- Scores from tricks plus item bonuses and penalties.

### Collector
- Setup: place the Reserve token next to your character card.
- You may reserve 1 card in a trick after playing your card; if you win the trick, you collect the reserved card.
- You cannot gain gold or black crowns.
- End of round: make up to 3 combinations from collected cards.
- Only 1 Rare card may be used per combination.
- White Flags and the Berserker card cannot be used in combinations.
- You lose points for every 2 unused cards.

### Ruler
- Setup: place the Reserve token next to your character card.
- Setup: give task cards to the other players.
- You may reserve 1 card in a trick after playing your card; if you win the trick, you collect the reserved card.
- You cannot gain gold or black crowns.
- You score from completed tasks; failed tasks penalize the task owner, not the Ruler.

## 2-Player Notes
- 2-player basic games use 5 rounds.
- Crowns are used to track character selection, not round rewards.
- In rounds 1 and 3, the gold-crown player selects first.
- In rounds 2 and 4, the black-crown player selects first.
- Each character can only be selected once in the whole game.
- In the final round, the player with fewer points chooses who leads first.

## Key Reminders
- Character text overrides the general rules if there is a conflict.
- If multiple win conditions happen at once, the earlier condition wins.
- If multiple players achieve the same win condition at once, higher character priority wins.
